Open source · Local-first · Bring your own keys

Your AI team, on your machine.

OpenBot is a desktop app for a small team of persistent AI Bots — running on Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI or local models. A Chief of Staff takes your requests, does the work or hands it to the right Bot, and only interrupts you when there's a result or it needs you.

MIT licensed · No hosted backend · Your keys never leave your machine

Why OpenBot

Most AI agent tools ask you to hand over your keys to someone else's server. OpenBot doesn't.

Build a small AI team

Give each Bot a role, an engine and model, permissions, connected tools, and — if it needs one — a computer.

Delegate through a Chief of Staff

It creates a new Bot only when an existing one can't do the job, hands off tasks, and keeps chatter out of your way.

Approve what matters

Reads and edits in the shared workspace just happen. Anything with side effects outside it asks first — plain words, exact command, risk level.

Bots can use a browser

With Docker Desktop, Bots drive Chromium on a shared Linux desktop. Watch every step live, or take over any time.

Automate on a schedule

Routines run a Bot's instructions every weekday at 8:00 or on an event — dry runs until you turn on live runs.

Stay in the loop from your phone

Pair a phone with a QR code over Wi-Fi, Tailscale or a Cloudflare Tunnel. Traffic is end-to-end encrypted.

See it work

Approvals, activity, routines and connectors — all in plain language, all local.

Approvals land in the chat, with the exact command and a risk level.
Watch a Bot's virtual computer live, or take over the mouse and keyboard.
